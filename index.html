About the author
Barry Floore
Barry Floore is a writer, composer, and independent producer in Jacksonville, Florida. He came to fiction by an unusual route — urban planning, nearly two decades of emergency and critical care nursing, and later work with language models. Years of watching people in extraordinary circumstances, and the ordinary ways they respond.

His work moves between dark comedy, satire, and tragedy, and keeps returning to loneliness, power, and the stories people tell themselves to make life bearable. He is most interested in characters who are neither entirely innocent nor entirely responsible for the circumstances they find themselves in. He believes even the darkest stories have something funny hiding in them.
